About Me

I am a student specializing in Mobile App and Web Development at STI College Lipa. I have a passion for creating high-performance applications and websites that combine sleek design with robust functionality.

My expertise extends into the world of Internet of Things (IoT), where I design and build smart hardware solutions like automated health gates and interactive display systems. I enjoy the challenge of integrating physical sensors and microcontrollers with cloud-based software to create seamless, real-world experiences.

Beyond development, I have a strong foundation in Networking and Cybersecurity, backed by numerous certifications in network troubleshooting, endpoint security, and ethical hacking. I bridge the gap between complex backend logic, secure infrastructure, and engaging frontend interfaces.

With 3 years of experience in HTML/CSS and 2 years in both Java and JavaScript, I focus on building secure, scalable, and user-centric digital solutions.
